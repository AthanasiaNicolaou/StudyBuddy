# StudyBuddy
Student-focused study partner matching app concept developed for EduDataHack 2025 — 1st Place

<h1>StudyBuddy</h1>
<h4> 🏆 1st Place — EduDataHack 2025 Local Hackathon </4>
StudyBuddy is a student-centred academic support application concept designed to help students develop better study habits through compatible study partnerships.

Developed by **LearnLink**, a two-person team, during the EduDataHack 2025 hackathon.

<h3>💡 The Problem</h3>
Students can face challenges such as:

- Difficulty maintaining focus
- Inefficient study methods
- Lack of motivation when studying alone
- Difficulty finding peers with similar study goals, subjects, availability and learning styles

These challenges can contribute to lower productivity, reduced engagement and poorer academic outcomes.

<h3>🚀 Our Solution</h3>
StudyBuddy aims to make it easier for students to find compatible study partners and build more effective study habits through collaboration and accountability.

The proposed application would allow students to:

1. Build a profile
2. Select their study preferences
3. Match with a compatible StudyBuddy
4. Schedule study sessions and track progress

The concept also includes a smart matching algorithm designed to identify suitable study partners based on students' preferences and goals.

<h3>✨ Key Features</h3>

- 🤝 Smart matching — Connect students with compatible study partners
- 📅 Study scheduling — Organise and plan study sessions
- 📈 Progress tracking — Monitor study activity and progress
- 🔒 Privacy & security — Account protection, data privacy and safe communication
- 🎓 Student-focused environment — Designed specifically for academic collaboration rather than general social networking
- 💎 Free and premium options — Different levels of access to the platform

<h3>🌍 Potential Impact</h3>
StudyBuddy was designed to:

- Encourage collaboration and accountability
- Support students in developing better study habits
- Create a supportive learning community
- Help students improve their academic performance
- Provide opportunities for home-schooled students to gain additional social interaction through academic collaboration

<h3>🛠️ Implementation & Future Potential</h3>

During the hackathon, we developed the concept, proposed implementation approach and explored the future potential of StudyBuddy as a student-focused platform.
